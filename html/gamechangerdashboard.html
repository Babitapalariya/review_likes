GameChanger Policies

1. Purpose

GameChanger empowers users to challenge, verify, and improve product claims through transparent evidence and community evaluation. Rewards and reputation are granted for genuine, verified contributions.

2. Participant Roles
  • Merchant: Uploads products and manages product claims.
  • GameChanger: Challenges claims with standardized methods and evidence.
  • Audience: Votes, comments, and supports genuine findings.
3. Claim Protocol

Each claim passes through three stages:

  • Under Review: Awaiting verification and moderation.
  • Voting Phase: Evaluated by community with weighted votes.
  • Resolved: Decision finalized and rewards released.
4. Rewards & Reputation
  • Earn credits, badges, and leaderboard visibility for valid claims.
  • Reputation grows with verified challenges and positive votes.
  • Low-quality or fake claims reduce reputation and reward eligibility.
5. Moderation & Conduct
  • All claims undergo moderation for accuracy and ethics.
  • False or harmful submissions may lead to suspension.
  • Each user is entitled to one appeal per moderation case.
6. Data Privacy

GameChanger protects user privacy and uses submitted content only for verification, research, and transparency reports. Personal details remain encrypted.
